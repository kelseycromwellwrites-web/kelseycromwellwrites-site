Lancashire Briefs in Lockdown

Written by Kelsey Cromwell Performed by Lancashire People’s Theatre
● Created During Lockdown 2020

When theatres closed during lockdown, Lancashire People’s Theatre kept the stage alive online. Kelsey wrote a series of original monologues for their actresses — intimate, character-driven pieces performed direct to camera that captured the isolation, humour, and resilience of everyday women during an extraordinary time.

The pieces were shared as part of the Lancashire Briefs in Lockdown series on social media, reaching audiences across the county and beyond during a period when live performance had gone dark.
