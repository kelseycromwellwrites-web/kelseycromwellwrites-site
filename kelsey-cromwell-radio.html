The Vicar’s Wife

Written by Kelsey Cromwell Directed by Olivia Lewis-Brown Produced by Magnus Albion Productions Limited

A story about following gut instinct, strength & silver linings.

A witty and heartfelt audio drama exploring what happens when life pulls the rug out from under you — and what it takes to find your footing again. Packed with warmth, humour, and hard-won wisdom, The Vicar’s Wife follows a woman navigating upheaval with equal parts grace and chaos.
