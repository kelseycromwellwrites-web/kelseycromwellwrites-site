Published Work
In the Nude
Olympia Publishers · March 2026

Lana Cotton takes a long-weekend trip to reunite with her long-term boyfriend, Luke Winning, only to discover he has posted her most intimate photos in a group chat with his new colleagues. The disloyalty pushes Lana into panic, shame and legal uncertainty, but a chance friendship with feisty Londoner Martha sparks something severe.

What began as a plan to delete the evidence shifts as Lana digs deeper — uncovering silent bystanders, hidden victims and a twisted sense of loyalty amongst the guilty. Caught in a web of betrayal, courage and moral complexity, she must face Luke’s deceit and discover her own strength. With Martha by her side, she learns that even in violation, resilience and friendship can guide her towards empowerment — and justice is worth fighting for.

Burn This If You Want
Young Adult Contemporary Fiction

Noah Hollins is writing to his brother Clarke from a hospital bed with a fifty-percent chance of walking out alive. For twenty years, he’s carried in his conscience what he has to say — a confession he always meant to give and never did, addressed to the one person he loves most and has spent half his life betraying.

It’s the story of how he got everything he thought he wanted at his brother’s expense — and how much more he actually lost in the taking. Now, with the ending closer than either of them is ready for: will Clarke read the whole truth, or watch it burn?

About

Storytelling with soul

Based in Salford, Manchester, Kelsey Cromwell is an award-winning writer whose work spans short film, theatre, and radio. With a Masters in Screenwriting from the University of Central Lancashire, she brings both formal craft and genuine human empathy to every project.

Her writing highlights darker aspects of the real world, often explored through happy-go-lucky characters — finding humour, optimism and resilience even in the grittiest circumstances. Emotionally raw yet grounded in truth, her stories challenge audiences to find hope in the most unlikely places.
